# DRAFT TEMPLATE: Reservation & Cancellation Policy

> **NOTICE:** Operational guidelines template for restaurant table management.

---

## 1. Booking Terms
- **Lead Time:** Reservations open 30 days in advance at 09:00 Paris Time (CET).
- **Party Sizes:** Tables for up to 8 guests can be requested online. Parties of 9 or more must contact our private salon concierge.
- **Grace Period:** Tables are held for 15 minutes past the reserved seating time before being released to walk-in guests.

## 2. Cancellation & Modification
- Guests may modify or cancel bookings without penalty up to **12 hours** prior to the reserved seating time.
- Late cancellations or no-shows impact our artisan purveyors and brigade; in production, credit card guarantees may apply a standard cover fee for unannounced absences.

## 3. Children & Dietary Requirements
- We warmly accommodate children with bespoke tasting portions upon advance notice.
- Dietary restrictions (allergens, vegetarian, vegan, religious diets) should be specified in the reservation notes so our chefs may tailor preparations.
