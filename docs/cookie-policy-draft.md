# DRAFT TEMPLATE: Cookie & Tracking Policy

> **NOTICE:** This document outlines the cookie footprint of Maison Étoile. Currently, this website operates in zero-tracking mode with zero non-essential cookies.

---

## 1. What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small alphanumeric files transferred to your computer or mobile device when accessing web applications.

## 2. Our Zero-Tracking Commitment
Maison Étoile respects customer discretion. By default:
- **No Third-Party Advertising Trackers:** We do not embed ad network pixels (Google Ads, Meta Pixel, TikTok).
- **No Invasive Analytics:** We do not track cross-site browsing behavior.
- **Strictly Essential Elements Only:** Any browser memory utilized is strictly for application rendering and session states (such as managing modal display).

## 3. Potential Future Analytics (Opt-In Architecture)
If privacy-friendly analytics (e.g., Plausible or Matomo configured with IP anonymization) are introduced in production:
1. No cookies will be set without affirmative user consent.
2. A compliant cookie banner adhering to CNIL guidelines will be rendered prior to tag execution.
