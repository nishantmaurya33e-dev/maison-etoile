## 2. Scope & Applicable Law
This policy outlines how personal data will be collected, stored, processed, and safeguarded under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - Regulation 2016/679) and the French *Loi Informatique et Libertés*.

## 3. Categories of Data Collected
During table booking and inquiry operations:
1. **Identification Data:** Full name, title.
2. **Contact Information:** Email address, telephone number (for SMS confirmations).
3. **Dining Preferences & Health-related Data:** Dietary restrictions, allergen declarations, mobility access requirements (processed only with explicit guest consent for culinary safety).

## 4. Purposes & Legal Bases of Processing
| Purpose | Data Processed | Legal Basis (GDPR Art. 6) |
|---|---|---|
| Reservation Management | Name, contact info, party size, date/time | Performance of a contract |
| Allergen & Dietary Safety | Guest dietary disclosures | Explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)) |
| Customer Service Inquiries | Email correspondence | Legitimate interest |
| Legal Record Keeping | Invoicing / Tax receipts | Legal obligation |

## 5. Demonstration Mode Notice
In the present digital portfolio build, no personal data entered into the reservation form is transmitted across public networks, persisted in browser databases, or stored in server files.
