# Data Retention & Deletion Policy (Draft)

## 1. Principles
Maison Étoile adheres to data minimization and storage limitation principles under GDPR Article 5(1)(e).

## 2. Retention Schedules
| Data Category | Purpose | Retention Window | Disposition Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reservation Inquiries | Table booking & guest service | 30 days post-dining date | Automated database purge |
| Allergen Disclosures | Kitchen safety during seating | Deleted immediately after service completion | Purged from active kitchen displays |
| Invoices & Receipts | French tax & accounting compliance | 10 years (*Code de commerce*) | Encrypted cold storage archive |
| Support Inquiries | Guest correspondence | 90 days after issue resolution | Secure deletion |

## 3. Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")
Guests may submit an erasure request at any time to `privacy@maisonetoile.example`. Upon verification, all non-statutory records will be erased within 30 calendar days.
